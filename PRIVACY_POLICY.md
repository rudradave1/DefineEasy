# DefineEasy Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: July 25, 2025**

## Overview

DefineEasy ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the DefineEasy mobile application (the "App"). The App is an offline-first dictionary designed for Indian exam students preparing for competitive exams.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

- **Search Queries**: Words you look up in the dictionary are stored locally on your device to improve your experience (search history).
- **Favorites and Collections**: Words you save as favorites or add to custom collections are stored locally.
- **Review Progress**: Your spaced-repetition review data (ratings, intervals, repetitions) is stored locally.

### Information Collected Automatically

- **Crash Reports**: If you opt in, we collect anonymized crash data via Firebase Crashlytics to improve app stability. This includes device model, OS version, and time of crash — never personal identifiers.
- **Analytics**: We collect anonymized usage analytics via Firebase Analytics to understand feature engagement (e.g., searches performed, reviews completed, quiz scores). This data is aggregated and cannot identify you personally.
- **Installation Identifiers**: Firebase may collect an Instance ID (a random, resettable identifier) to associate analytics events and crash reports with a single installation without linking to your identity.

## How We Use Your Information

- To provide dictionary search and word definitions
- To enable spaced-repetition review and progress tracking
- To improve app features and fix bugs based on aggregate usage data
- To send optional review reminders (only if you enable notifications)

## Data Storage and Security

- **Offline-First**: All your personal data (search history, favorites, review progress) is stored locally on your device using Room database (SQLite). It never leaves your device.
- **No Cloud Sync**: We do not sync your personal data to any server. There is no cloud backup of your favorites, collections, or review history.
- **No Account Required**: DefineEasy does not require an account, registration, or login. We have no access to your identity.
- **Firebase Data**: Crashlytics and Analytics data is stored on Google's servers in accordance with Firebase's security practices. This data is anonymized and aggregated.

## Third-Party Services

### Firebase (Google)
- **Firebase Analytics**: [Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- **Firebase Crashlytics**: [Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

Firebase services are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Data collected includes anonymized usage events, crash traces, and device information. Firebase may process this data in the United States or other countries where Google maintains servers.

### Dictionary API
When you search for a word that is not cached locally, the App fetches word definitions from a third-party dictionary API. The API receives only the word being searched — no personal information is transmitted.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Since all personal data is stored locally on your device, you retain full control:

- **Delete Search History**: Settings → Clear Search History
- **Delete All Favorites**: Settings → Clear All Favorites
- **Reset Review Progress**: Settings → Reset Review Progress
- **Full Data Deletion**: Uninstall the App to remove all locally stored data.
- **Firebase Data**: Analytics and crash data retained per Google's retention policies (typically 14 months for analytics, 90 days for crash reports). Contact us to request deletion.

## Your Rights (India — DPDP Act, 2023)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

- **Access**: Know what data we hold (though we hold none of your personal data server-side).
- **Correction**: Since data is local, you can modify or delete it directly in the App.
- **Erasure**: Uninstall the App to delete all local data, or contact us for Firebase data deletion.
- **Grievance Redressal**: Contact our Data Protection Officer using the details below.

## Children's Privacy

DefineEasy is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Changes are effective when posted. We encourage you to review this policy regularly.
